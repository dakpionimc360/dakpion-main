About Dakpion IMC

Cultivating ideas. Crafting success.

We are an integrated marketing communications agency from Dhaka, built on a simple belief: brands grow fastest when every touchpoint works together.

Our story

From a creative studio to a 360° agency

Dakpion started as a small creative and digital studio, and grew by helping healthcare professionals across Bangladesh build trusted brands online. Along the way our clients kept asking for more — TV commercials, billboards, events, media buying.

So we built it. Today Dakpion IMC brings strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers, media planners, developers and activation teams under one roof — delivering integrated campaigns for brands across healthcare, FMCG, real estate, education, e-commerce and more.

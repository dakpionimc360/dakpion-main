About Dakpion IMC

Cultivating Ideas, Crafting Success — a 360° integrated marketing communications agency in Dhaka.

Our story

From a creative studio to a full 360° agency

Dakpion started as a small creative and digital studio, helping doctors, clinics and hospitals build trusted brands online. As clients grew, they asked for more — TV commercials, billboards, events and media buying.

So we built it. Today strategists, designers, writers, video producers, media planners, developers and event teams work together under one roof.

About Dakpion IMC

Cultivating Ideas, Crafting Success. We’re a 360° integrated marketing communications agency based in Dhaka.

Our story

From a creative studio to a full 360° agency

Dakpion started as a small creative and digital studio, helping doctors, clinics and hospitals across Bangladesh build trusted brands online. As our clients grew, they asked for more — TV commercials, billboards, events and media buying.

So we built it. Today our strategists, designers, writers, video producers, media planners, developers and event teams work together under one roof for brands in healthcare, FMCG, real estate, education, e-commerce and more.
